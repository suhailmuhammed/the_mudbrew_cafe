## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an earthy, tactile, and mindful luxury inspired by the warmth of raw clay, pottery studios, and slow artisanal coffee brewing. The aesthetic balances handcrafted organic forms with disciplined architectural restraint. It is designed for creative urbanites, culinary explorers, and craft enthusiasts seeking an intentional, calming space that merges studio art with experiential dining.

The overall movement blends **Tactile / Skeuomorphic subtleties** with **Earthy Editorial Minimalism**. Rather than flat digital neutrality, surfaces evoke unglazed ceramic bisque, soft natural lime plaster, and warm morning backlighting reminiscent of the cafe's cove niches. Interfaces must feel grounded, tranquil, and distinctly physical—inviting the user to browse workshop bookings, seasonal artisanal menus, and handcrafted pottery collections with unhurried delight.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects natural studio elements: oxidized terracotta clay, soothing botanical sage, sun-baked sandstone warmth, and espresso-washed charcoal.

- **Primary (`#B85434`)**: Rich fired terracotta clay. Reserved for focal interaction points, hero call-to-actions, highlight badges, and primary branding elements.
- **Secondary (`#4D6B53`)**: Muted deep sage. Evokes leafy cafe greenery and herbal garden notes; used for secondary actions, confirmed states, studio pottery tags, and calming accents.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B52`)**: Golden bisque / warm amber. Sourced from the cafe's back-lit architectural cove niches; used for soft focus highlights, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Neutral (`#271E18`)**: Deep roasted espresso charcoal. Used for primary typography, crisp icon outlines, and grounding structures, replacing harsh black with a rich, roasted organic tone.
- **Backgrounds & Canvases**: Surface foundations use warm sand cream (`#FAF6F0`) and smooth clay slip (`#F3ECE2`), ensuring every screen radiates hospitality rather than stark sterile white.

## Typography

The typography pairings contrast literary artisanal craftsmanship with modern utilitarian clarity. 

- **Headlines & Display (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys hospitality, culinary tradition, and handcrafted sculpture. Headlines should use generous line height and tight letter-spacing to accentuate the serif curves. Italic variations are encouraged for decorative sub-quotes, origin descriptions, and workshop callouts.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Inter`)**: Grounds the interface in flawless legibility. Used across interactive elements, table layouts, menu pricing, booking schedulers, and instructions. The neutral geometry balances the rich personality of the serif display type.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Small headers and category tags leverage uppercase styling with slightly expanded letter tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) to provide clear scanning over tinted clay backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the spatial balance of an architectural ceramic gallery: open, uncrowded, and deliberate. 

- **Grid Model**: A standard 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width 1280px) with 24px gutters; an 8-column layout on tablet with 20px gutters; and a 4-column responsive grid on mobile devices with 16px gutters and 20px edge margins.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace**: Generous vertical pacing allows artisan photography and pottery wares to stand out without competing with navigation. Spacing between major content sections relies on relaxed multiples of `space-xl` (40px–60px) to prevent cognitive overload.
- **Asymmetrical Compositions**: Hero galleries and culinary highlights may span staggered offsets (e.g., 7 columns text / 5 columns image), evoking the curated alcove shelving seen throughout the studio interior.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm illumination** and **subtle tactile layering**, inspired by the cafe's glowing plaster wall sconces and illuminated display arches:

- **Surface Tiers**: Elements stack gently from the deep sand base (`#FAF6F0`) to warm ceramic white tiles (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted clay slabs (`#F3ECE2`).
- **Warm Ambient Glow**: Cast shadows avoid standard cool grays. Instead, they use a soft, warm umber tint (`rgba(68, 44, 30, 0.08)`) with wide diffusion (16px–32px blur) to simulate soft indirect ambient lighting hitting textured plaster.
- **Architectural Niche Depth**: Inner cutouts and search bars feature a very subtle soft inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(39, 30, 24, 0.04)`) to evoke recessed plaster alcoves.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Replaced where possible with tonal shifts; when structural separation is necessary, use a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(184, 84, 52, 0.12)`) in dilute terracotta.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary takes its cue from hand-thrown ceramics, arched wall alcoves, and smoothed clay edges:

- **Curvature Hierarchy**: Standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, notification badges) utilize gentle rounded curves (8px / `0.5rem`).
- **Feature Cards & Surfaces**: Content cards and booking panels scale up to softer radii (16px / `1rem` up to 24px / `1.5rem`).
- **The Ceramic Arch Motif**: Key hero banners, portrait imagery, and workshop highlights may adopt prominent architectural arch silhouettes (rounded top corners at 120px+ with flat base corners), directly echoing the cafe's signature interior wall cutouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Terracotta background (`#B85434`), crisp cream text (`#FAF6F0`), 8px border radius, 12px 24px padding. Hover states deepen to baked brick (`#A04325`) with a soft warm elevation glow.
- **Secondary (Craft / Sage)**: Deep sage fill (`#4D6B53`) with cream text, used for experiential bookings (pottery wheels, kiln firing slots).
- **Outlined / Ghost**: 1.5px border in `#B85434` with espresso charcoal text, turning terracotta-tinted on hover.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Selection State**: Active chips use terracotta with white text; unselected chips sit on a soft sand slip (`#F3ECE2`) with neutral espresso text and no outer border. Roundedness is pill-styled (full round) for tactile touch targets.

### Input Fields
- Recessed sand slip background (`#F5EFE6`) with dark espresso text. No heavy border; an unselected field has a 1px border of `rgba(39, 30, 24, 0.1)`. On focus, transitions cleanly to a 1.5px solid terracotta ring with a subtle warm glow. Placeholder text is muted roasted taupe.

### Cards (Menu & Workshop Items)
- Built on crisp ceramic cream tiles (`#FFFFFF`) with 16px corner radius and ambient umber shadows. Cards feature an image container with matching curvature, high-contrast serif item titles (`Playfair Display`), concise descriptions, and secondary action buttons or reservation time tags.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Subtle ceramic pill checkmarks and discs. Inactive border in warm taupe (`rgba(39, 30, 24, 0.3)`); selected fill transitions smoothly to solid terracotta (`#B85434`) with an ivory interior indicator.

### Additional Experiential Components
- **Workshop Time-Slot Selector**: Horizontal scroll tiles showing kiln studio session times, marked with live wheel availability chips in muted sage.
- **Artisan Material Badges**: Tactile tags specifying clay body (e.g., *Stoneware*, *Terracotta*, *Porcelain*) using label-sm typography with delicate rounded borders.
